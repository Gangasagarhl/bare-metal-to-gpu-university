# Post-mortem: kv writes failed for 21 minutes (revised draft)

## Summary
From 02:14 to 02:34 every write to the kv service failed (12,600 requests); reads were not
affected. The leader kv-1 ran out of disk space during the nightly compaction, which writes
the new segment before deleting the old ones and left a temporary file behind when it failed.
A full disk on the leader stops writes but not heartbeats, so no follower took over.

## Impact
21 minutes x 600 writes per minute = 12,600 failed writes [metrics]. 0 failed reads [metrics].
kv-1 lagged behind the other replicas from 02:37 to 03:01, so the service ran on 2 of 3 replicas.

## Timeline
- 3 days before: wal retention raised from 4 to 8 segments; disk 60 % -> 78 % [change log]
- 02:00 compaction starts on kv-1 [log kv-1]
- 02:11 ticket: kv-1 disk above 95 %, routed to a business-hours queue [alerts]
- 02:14 kv-1 wal.append ENOSPC; all writes fail [log kv-1, metrics]
- 02:14 page to primary on-call [alerts]
- 02:21 page acknowledged (7 minutes after the page) [alerts]
- 02:26 SEV2 declared; IC rui; ops mia paged [chat]
- 02:34 kv-1 restarted to move leadership; kv-2 elected [chat, log kv-2]
- 02:35 writes succeed again [metrics]
- 03:01 temporary file deleted on kv-1; kv-1 catches up [log kv-1, log kv-2]
- 03:05 resolved [chat]

## Root cause and contributing factors
Trigger: compaction needs free space equal to the data it rewrites, and it does not remove
its temporary file when it fails [log kv-1].
Contributing: the retention change three days earlier raised the baseline disk use to 78 %,
so the compaction no longer fit [change log]. The disk alert existed but was a ticket for
business hours, so nobody saw it at 02:11 [alerts]. A full disk on the leader is a partial
failure the election does not detect: heartbeats continue while writes fail [traces].
The runbook step "restart the leader" mitigated the outage but did not mention the disk.

## Detection
The write-error page fired at 02:14, at the first failing minute. The disk ticket at 02:11
was the earliest signal but was not routed to a human at night.

## Response and recovery
Roles were declared at 02:26. The IC checked that kv-2 and kv-3 were in sync before the
restart (02:31). Recovery of full redundancy waited 26 minutes for diagnosis of the disk.

## What went well
The risk check before the restart. One person changed production at a time.

## What went wrong
Seven minutes passed between the page and its acknowledgement; the pager log does not show why.
The disk signal existed 3 minutes before the outage but went to a daytime queue.

## Where we got lucky
From 02:37 to 03:01 one more replica failure would have stopped writes again.

## Action items
- (prevent) compaction checks free space first and removes its temp file on failure; owner: mia; due: 2026-10-24
- (prevent) capacity review required for retention changes; owner: storage team lead; due: 2026-10-31
- (detect) page when leader disk above 95 % at night; owner: rui; due: 2026-10-17
- (mitigate) leader steps down when its WAL cannot append for 30 s; owner: kv maintainers; due: 2026-11-14
- (process) runbook "kv writes failing": add disk check before restart; owner: mia; due: 2026-10-17

## Lessons
A leader can be alive and useless at the same time; health checks must test the work, not
the heartbeat.
