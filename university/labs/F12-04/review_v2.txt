@ rtc.cc:47 [blocking]
This wait, the one on line 50 and the for(;;) on line 49 have no bound. If the status
register ever keeps bit 7 set (a chip that is absent or broken), the caller hangs
here without a message, because the loop has no exit. Our design table asks
for a timeout on every wait (curriculum 5.4, row "Error handling"). Could read_raw()
give up after a fixed number of polls and return a failure the caller can report?

@ rtc_decode.h:19 [suggestion]
The call rtc_decode(modes[0], false) does not say what false means at the call
