# DN301 — Estimation and control for drones: author notes

Chapters F10-13 to F10-19, L3–L4, 4 credits. Prerequisites: RB302, DN202, MA301.
Levels: F10-13 L3, F10-14 L3, F10-15 L4, F10-16 L3, F10-17 L3, F10-18 L4, F10-19 L4.
Labs are in `university/labs/F10-13` to `university/labs/F10-19`.

Each lab passes `university/labs/run_lab.sh university/labs/<ID>` with exit code 0. All seven were re-run at the end of this build, on 2026-10-10, in order: F10-13 first, because the later labs read its recording.

Every number in the prose, figures, worked examples and answer keys comes from one of two places:
- printed by a listing, or
- recomputed by that chapter's `checks.cpp`.

The figures are generated by scripts that parse the `.out` files. "Predict, then run" answers that need a modified listing say so, and give no number.

## Toolchain and local evidence

- **Compiler.** g++ (Ubuntu 13.3.0-6ubuntu2~24.04.1) 13.3.0, with flags `-std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror -g -fsanitize=address,undefined`. Sources are formatted with clang-format (LLVM base, 4-space indent, 100 columns, left pointer alignment).
- **No hardware.** This build had no IMU, GNSS receiver, flight controller, ESC or vehicle. All data come from simulation:
  - the DN201 simulator (`labs/F10-04/quadsim.hpp`, course quad: 1 kg, 0.15 m arms, motor time constant 30 ms);
  - modelled sensors written in the listings.

  The F10-13 recording uses:
  - gyro bias (0.004, −0.006, 0.003) rad/s and gyro noise 0.003 rad/s;
  - accelerometer noise 0.25 m/s²;
  - GNSS position σ 0.5 m and velocity σ 0.1 m/s;
  - seed 20261010.
- **No PX4, ArduPilot, Gazebo or SITL** were installed or run. Their details appear only in unverified boxes.
- **Figure check.** The figures were rendered with cairosvg in scratch space, using stand-in CSS for the sv-* classes, and checked by eye. A check in the real site theme is still needed.
- **No timings, no GPU.**

## Claims resting on sources that were not opened (dossier gate G1 open)

- **B1 (all chapters): Beard and McLain, "Small Unmanned Aircraft: Theory and Practice"** (registry 4.8). Cited for:
  - attitude estimation with gyros and accelerometers (F10-13, F10-14);
  - EKF and GPS-aided navigation (F10-15);
  - successive loop closure and bandwidth separation, including the commonly quoted 3–10 ratio (F10-16);
  - loop design (F10-17 to F10-19).

  The Source Researcher must confirm the edition and sections.
- **B2 (F10-13, F10-14): Mahony, Hamel and Pflimlin, "Nonlinear Complementary Filters on the Special Orthogonal Group"** (IEEE TAC, 2008). Cited for:
  - the explicit complementary filter on SO(3);
  - the cross-product error;
  - the bias-estimating version and its stability results.

  The form of the filter equations in Listing 1 of F10-14 is this build's own; it should be compared with the paper.
- **D2 and D3 (all chapters): PX4 Autopilot User Guide (including EKF2, control allocation, PID tuning, SITL and log-analysis pages) and the ArduPilot documentation.** Title only; nothing was opened.
- **D1 (each chapter).** Covers the derivations shown in the chapter, recomputed by its runs. The general design remarks it also covers (error-state filters, delayed fusion, allocation methods, frequency-domain identification, motor heating from command noise) are background knowledge that still needs a source.

## Analogy (F10 world: carrying a tray of drinks while walking)

Registered mappings used:
- attitude estimation (F10-13);
- complementary filter (F10-14);
- cascaded control, as manager → team leader → worker → hands (F10-16).

**Proposals for the analogy registry** (used in the chapters, not registered yet):
- **EKF / GNSS aiding (F10-15):** a friend walking beside Amara who calls out her walking speed, which lets her subtract her own push from what she feels.
- **Rate-loop gains (F10-17):** how strongly Amara's hands react to a wobble they feel. D is reacting to how fast the tray starts to tip. I is the slow habit of leaning more on the heavier side.
- **Allocation priority (F10-18):** which glass Amara saves when she cannot save all. This reuses DN201 F10-02's "four fingertips under the tray" for the mixer.
- **Oscillation and windup (F10-19):**
  - oscillation: hands reacting so hard and so late that they chase their own corrections;
  - windup: a hand that kept pressing against a sloping counter, released at lift.

DN301's "Observability" was renamed "Observability (of a state)" so that it does not merge with DS402's monitoring sense of the word.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Forensic evidence format.** The forensic logs (including the course forensic "Oscillation") are printed by the course's own programs in a plain-text table, not real PX4 ULog or ArduPilot DataFlash files. When PX4 SITL is available, regenerate them as real logs and add an exercise on reading those tools' formats.
2. **PX4 SITL lab.** The course card asks for "PX4 SITL tuning". It is written as an optional, untested part B in F10-17, with all names in an unverified box. It needs a build environment with PX4 and a simulator, and a verified parameter list.
3. **Recording size.** `F10-13/imu_flight.csv` is about 183 KB, generated by a listing at lab time rather than stored as a fixture. The owner may prefer a smaller recording (for example 50 Hz) or a committed copy.
4. **Gains and loop rates.** The cascade's gains and loop rates (500/250/50 Hz) are this course's exercise values for the simulated course quad. They are not autopilot defaults, and the chapters say so.
5. **Delay value.** The 6 ms loop delay in F10-19 is an exercise value; a measured delay from a real vehicle would make the margin table concrete.
6. **Dependency on DN201's simulator.** Every DN301 lab needs `labs/F10-04/quadsim.hpp`. Any interface change there breaks DN301; consider freezing that interface.
