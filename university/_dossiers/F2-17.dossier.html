C. Not found / not opened

  • The Core Guidelines have no general rule "prefer explicit captures"; F.54 covers the [=]-in-member-function case. The chapter now presents the general preference as the course's own.
  • Whether g++ uses the ABI's per-signature numbering for lambdas in main (the ABI leaves TU-local closures to the compiler), and how the demangler prints it: not checked; unverified box.
  • Inlining of std::function calls at -O2 and their cost: not measured; unverified box.
  • The AddressSanitizer wiki page says use-after-return detection needs ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_stack_use_after_return=1, while the Clang page says it is enabled by default on Linux; R5 shows the check active with g++ 13.3.0. Recorded as a conflict resolved by the observed run (guide 12.7).
