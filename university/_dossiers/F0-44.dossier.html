C. Fact list

  • F1 An H-bridge driver selects forward, reverse, coast or brake from two inputs; the inputs can carry PWM for speed. Source: D4 §7.3.2. Used in: Jargon (Motor, H-bridge); Layers 1–2; Layer 3; Figure 1.
  • F2 The motor supply (VM) is rated separately from the digital inputs. Source: D4 §6.3, §9.2. Used in: Jargon (Motor driver).
  • F3 Internal dead time prevents cross-conduction (shoot-through). Source: D4 §6.5, §8.2. Used in: Layer 3; Breaks 1; Layer 1 rule.
  • F4 Because of the motor's inductance its current must keep flowing; it recirculates through the driver. Source: D4 §7.3.2. Used in: Layer 2 (second reason for a driver).
  • F5 An inductor's induced voltage opposes a change in current; large induced voltages can occur. Source: D6 §14.2. Used in: Layer 2; Breaks 2.
  • F6 A motor has loops of wire in a magnetic field; torque on them turns a shaft; the torque depends on the current direction. Source: D5 §22.8. Used in: Jargon (Motor); hardware.
  • F7 A motor draws its largest current when on but not turning, more at start-up and when a load slows it. Source: D5 §23.6. Used in: Layer 2; Breaks 3.
  • F8 A short circuit is a low-resistance path between the terminals of a source; its heat can damage or ignite materials. Source: D5 §20.6. Used in: Jargon (Short circuit); Layer 1.
  • F9 A small base current controls a much larger collector current; a transistor can work as an on-off switch. Source: D7 §9.7. Used in: Jargon (Transistor); Layer 2.
  • F10 An output pin can source or sink only a limited current and can be damaged; motors need a transistor or other interface circuitry; analogWrite writes a PWM wave of a given duty cycle. Source: D8. Used in: Layer 1; Jargon (Current limit, Motor driver); Layer 3.
  • F11 Current through a resistance produces heat. Source: D6 §9.5. Used in: Breaks 3.
  • F12 The reference kit names the :MOVE Motor (two bi-direction DC motors, AA pack). Source: K1. Used in: Code note; D3.
  • F13 driver_check, hbridge_sim and hbridge_log outputs; 7 of 16 switch settings are shorts (arithmetic). Source: R1, R2. Used in: Worked example; forensic.
