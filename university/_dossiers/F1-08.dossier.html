C. Facts (keyed to the chapter's claim tags)

  1. F1 [D4] Pending: values of the kit's parts and meter (no document yet). Used in: jargon, layer3, lab.
  2. F2 [D5 §10.3] Kirchhoff's junction rule (charge conservation at a node) and loop rule (the voltage changes around a loop add to zero). Used in: transition.
  3. F3 [D5 §10.5] RC charging q(t) = Cε(1 − e^−t/RC); τ = RC in seconds; 0.632 of the final value after one τ; discharge leaves 0.368 after one τ; initial current ε/R. Used in: l2.
  4. F4 [D5 §9.4] Ohm's law V = IR for ohmic components, R = V/I is the slope of the V–I line; Ohm's law is empirical, not universal; a diode is nonohmic, conducts in one direction, passes essentially no reverse current until breakdown. Used in: transition.
  5. F5 [D14] A Schmitt trigger switches at Vt+ on rising and Vt− on falling inputs; hysteresis = Vt+ − Vt−; noise on a slow input can cross one threshold many times; thresholds are given as min/max that depend on VCC. Used in: jargon, layer1, l2, layer3, hardware.
  6. F6 [D15] The 74HC14/74HCT14 transform slowly changing inputs into sharp, jitter-free outputs; VT+ and VT− are min–max ranges per VCC and part type; section 13 shows an R–C relaxation oscillator built from one inverter. Used in: jargon, l2, layer3.
  7. F7 [D16] VIH/VIL are the input voltage ranges recognised as logic 1/0; VOH/VOL are the output levels guaranteed; inputs must stay above VIH min or below VIL max. Used in: jargon, layer1, l2, breaks.
  8. F8 [D17] Noise margin examples: VIL − VOL = 0.7 − 0.4 = 300 mV; an interface can have no noise margin. Used in: layer1, l2.
  9. F9 [D18] Sampling takes values at discrete times; the sampling rate must be at least twice the highest signal frequency; otherwise aliasing makes signals appear at wrong, lower frequencies. Used in: jargon, layer1, breaks, transition.
  10. F10 [D19] An ideal converter's quantisation error is at most ±½ LSB: information within one step is lost. Used in: jargon, transition.
  11. F11 [R1] Outputs of this chapter's lab runs (R1 row in section B; toolchain in section E). Used in: l2, worked, code.
  12. F12 [S1] Guide 'Honesty first' safety statement (local file). Used in: lab.
