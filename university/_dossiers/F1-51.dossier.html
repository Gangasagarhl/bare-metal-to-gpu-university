C. Facts (keyed to the chapter's claim tags)

  1. F1 [D6 RGMII] RGMII needs a delay of about 1.5 ns to 2 ns between clock and data; it may be added by the PHY, by the MAC (if capable) or by the printed-circuit-board traces; the PHY interface modes are PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_RGMII (no delay in the PHY), _RGMII_TXID (PHY adds the transmit delay), _RGMII_RXID (receive delay) and _RGMII_ID (both), stated from the PHY's point of view; a MAC driver that sees any mode other than plain RGMII must disable its own delays. Used in: layer3 "RGMII and the delay", unverified box 1, forensic answer key, breaks
  2. F2 [D6 MDIO / adjust_link] PHY devices sit on an MDIO bus managed by the kernel; the MAC driver attaches to the PHY and is told of link changes through its adjust_link callback. Used in: jargon (MDIO, link up), layer2, summary
  3. F3 [R1] Listing 1 writes destination ff:ff:ff:ff:ff:ff, source 52:54:00:12:34:56, EtherType 0x0806 in network byte order, a 28-byte ARP request, zero padding to 60 bytes and a CRC-32 (reflected polynomial 0xEDB88320, initial 0xFFFFFFFF, final inversion), 64 bytes in total, saved as frame.pcap. Used in: pictures (Figure 2), layer2, worked example, code walk-through
  4. F4 [R2] tshark decodes the frame as "Destination: Broadcast", "Type: ARP (0x0806)", "Padding: 000000…", "Frame check sequence: 0x8b1fe63c [correct]", "[FCS Status: Good]", ARP request from 10.0.2.15 for 10.0.2.2, LG bit "Locally administered address", IG bit "Individual address (unicast)" / "Group address (multicast/broadcast)". Used in: jargon, layer2, worked example
  5. F5 [R3] zlib.crc32(b"123456789") = 0xcbf43926, the standard CRC-32 check value; Listing 1's crc32 returns the same. Used in: layer3 "The FCS is a CRC-32"
  6. F6 [R4] QEMU's e1000 capture of the iPXE ROM: DHCP Discover and the following frames as tshark lists them; frames are recorded by QEMU's back end without FCS. Used in: layer3 "Where a capture is taken", lab
  7. F7 [R5] Loopback capture: three UDP datagrams to 127.0.0.1 with source and destination 00:00:00:00:00:00 ("Globally unique address (factory default)", "Individual address"), no FCS present. Used in: layer3, worked example
  8. F8 [R6] Toy model: data changes every 4 ticks, samples within 1 tick of a change are unreliable, one delay switch adds 2 ticks; with both ends adding delay the sampled stream is corrupt while the "link" register still reads up. Used in: layer3, forensic pack (made-up register map, ruling A2)
  9. F9 [R7] Delay sweep: exactly one delay configuration yields error-free sampling in the model; link state never changes across the sweep. Used in: forensic pack and answer key

D. Not found / not opened

  1. N1 IEEE 802.3 (D1) was not opened: the minimum frame size of 64 bytes including FCS, the 1500-byte maximum standard payload, the preamble (7 bytes) and start-frame delimiter (1 byte), and the clause 22 register numbers (BMCR 0, BMSR 1 with link status, PHYID 2 and 3) remain in the chapter's unverified box. The run proves only that a 64-byte frame with a correct FCS decodes cleanly.
  2. N2 The RGMII specification (D2) was not opened; only the delay value and its placement (PHY, MAC or traces) are confirmed, from D6.
  3. N3 The devicetree spellings of the four RGMII modes ("rgmii", "rgmii-id", "rgmii-rxid", "rgmii-txid") were not checked against the Ethernet controller binding (Documentation/devicetree/bindings/net/ethernet-controller.yaml); the kernel-side names are confirmed (D6) and the spellings stay in the forensic key's unverified box.
  4. N4 The Wireshark User's Guide (D4) was not opened: the general rule about where padding and FCS are visible in OS captures stays in its unverified box; the chapter's three captures (R2, R4, R5) are the only evidence.
  5. N5 RFC 826 (ARP) and the IANA EtherType registry were not opened; the chapter states only what tshark printed ("Type: ARP (0x0806)", hardware type 1).

F. Hardware facts

No physical NIC or PHY was used. The e1000 of R4 is QEMU's emulation; the loopback interface of R5 has no PHY. The chapter's student step on a physical NIC and the ethtool statistics are marked untested and say what is needed (a Linux computer with a physical NIC, the ethtool tool, and permission to capture). Named PHYs with public datasheets are left to the curriculum catalog (AH-21).
