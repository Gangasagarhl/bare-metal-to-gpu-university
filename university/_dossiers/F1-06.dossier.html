C. Facts (keyed to the chapter's claim tags)

  1. F1 [D4] Pending: values of the kit's parts and meter (no document yet). Used in: jargon, layer1, l2, breaks, transition, lab.
  2. F2 [D5 §10.3] Kirchhoff's junction rule (charge conservation at a node) and loop rule (the voltage changes around a loop add to zero). Used in: l2, transition.
  3. F3 [D5 §9.4] Ohm's law V = IR for ohmic components, R = V/I is the slope of the V–I line; Ohm's law is empirical, not universal; a diode is nonohmic, conducts in one direction, passes essentially no reverse current until breakdown. Used in: jargon, layer1, layer3, transition.
  4. F4 [D7 §9.6] Donor impurities give n-type material (extra electrons); acceptor impurities give p-type (holes). Used in: hardware.
  5. F5 [D7 §9.7] Diode current I = I0(e^(eV/kBT) − 1); forward bias narrows the depletion layer, reverse bias widens it; reverse current is very small until breakdown; a junction transistor is built from n, p, n regions. Used in: jargon, layer1, l2, layer3, hardware, breaks.
  6. F6 [D11] LED forward voltage falls as the die warms, a positive feedback; in parallel strings the LED with the lowest forward voltage draws the most current. Used in: l2, layer3.
  7. F7 [D12] In an LED, electrons and holes meet and recombine, creating light; the wavelength (colour) depends on the semiconductor. Used in: jargon, hardware.
  8. F8 [R1] Outputs of this chapter's lab runs (R1 row in section B; toolchain in section E). Used in: jargon, layer1, l2, worked, code.
  9. F9 [S1] Guide 'Honesty first' safety statement (local file). Used in: lab.
