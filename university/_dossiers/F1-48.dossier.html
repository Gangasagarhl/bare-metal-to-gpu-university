C. Facts (keyed to the chapter's claim tags)

  1. F1 [D3 [basic.fundamental] ¶2] Unsigned arithmetic is performed modulo 2^N, so the difference of two wrapped unsigned counter values is the elapsed count as long as fewer than 2^N ticks passed. Confirmed. Used in: jargon (wrap-around), layer3 (wrap-safe comparison).
  2. F2 [D3 [time.clock.steady]] steady_clock's time points never decrease and the clock may not be adjusted (is_steady is true). Confirmed. Used in: layer3 (host clocks).
  3. F3 [D3 [thread.thread.this] ¶7] sleep_for blocks the calling thread for the relative timeout; the guarantee that the sleep lasts at least that long is stated in [thread.req.timing], which was not opened. The chapter's "at least 1 ms" is a measurement (R3), not quoted from the standard. Used in: layer3.
  4. F4 [D1] Edition and chapter titles confirmed on the catalogue page; the oscillator/PLL/counter text was not opened. Used in: layer1, layer2.
  5. F5 [D2, D4, D5, D6] Not checked: timer reload conventions, SysTick width and clock source, TSC/HPET/APIC timer behaviour, jiffies and wrap-safe macros. Stay in the unverified boxes / tagged by title. Used in: layer2, layer3.
