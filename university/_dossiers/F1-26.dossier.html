  2. F2 [R2] The sum program (30 executed instructions) takes 34 cycles in ideal mode; CPI 1.13. Used in: layer 2, summary.
  3. F3 [R3] stage_time: periods 850 and 270 du; speed-ups 0.63 (n = 1), 1.75 (5), 2.25 (10), 3.03 (100), 3.14 (1000), 3.15 (1,000,000); 271,080 du for 1000 instructions. Used in: worked example, forensic lab.
