C. Fact list

  • F1 A CdS photocell senses light; its resistance in the dark is much higher than in light. Source: D4. Used in: Jargon (Sensor); Layer 2 Light.
  • F2 In a series loop the same current flows, so with a fixed resistor the divider voltage follows the photocell's resistance. Source: D10 §9.4, §10.2. Used in: Layer 2 Light.
  • F3 A tilt switch uses a rolling ball that opens or closes its contacts depending on tilt. Source: D5. Used in: Layer 2 Tilt; Layer 1.
  • F4 A time-of-flight sensor emits infrared laser pulses that reflect from the target and are detected; it meets Class 1 laser limits and warns against focusing the beam with optics. Source: D6. Used in: Layer 2 Distance; Safety box.
  • F5 The time for echoes to return is proportional to the distance of the reflector; devices measure distance by timing ultrasonic echoes. Source: D7 §17.7. Used in: Layer 2 Distance.
  • F6 With noise, a single threshold gives unwanted output transitions; upper and lower thresholds (hysteresis) remove them. Source: D8. Used in: Jargon (Noise, Hysteresis); Layer 1; Layer 3.
  • F7 analogRead maps input voltages to whole numbers with a multichannel ADC whose resolution depends on the board; Smoothing averages several readings. Source: D9. Used in: Jargon (Analog and digital); Layer 3; hardware.
  • F8 The reference kit names the micro:bit V2 accelerometer and the :MOVE Motor ultrasonic and line sensors; no separate light sensor. Source: K1. Used in: Code note; D3.
  • F9 Sensor table outputs; flicker run; hysteresis run. Source: R1, R2. Used in: Worked example; forensic.
