C. Facts (keyed to the chapter's claim tags)

  1. F1 [D9a ch. 15] Coherent (consistent) mappings and streaming mappings; streaming mappings are set up for one direction and, where the hardware needs it, a bounce buffer is used; on non-coherent systems processor caches covering a DMA area must be invalidated before the CPU reads device-written data. Confirmed. Used in: jargon (bounce buffer), layer3 (coherence, mapping and sync).
  2. F2 [D9b] A streaming buffer is mapped with a direction and synced (dma_sync_single_for_cpu) before the CPU reads it; DMA buffers must not share a cache line with unrelated data (ARCH_DMA_MINALIGN); the descriptor example uses wmb() between writing descriptor words so the device sees them in order; dma_set_mask tells the kernel the device's address width. Confirmed. Used in: layer3 (sync, alignment, write barrier, address range).
  3. F3 [D9] Not checked: dma_wmb()/dma_rmb() and the ordering of writel() relative to earlier stores (Documentation/memory-barriers.txt was not opened); the chapter's "read barrier" phrase for descriptor status reads. Stay in the (narrowed) unverified box. Used in: layer3 (barriers).
  4. F4 [D8] Not checked: IOMMU per-device translation, blocking of unmapped accesses, interrupt remapping, translation caches. QEMU's intel-iommu address spaces (R4) are evidence of QEMU's model only. Used in: jargon, layer3.
  5. F5 [D2, D7] Catalogue page / not opened. Bus-master and memory-write-request claims unchecked at section level. Used in: layer2, layer3.
  6. F6 [L1] PCI_COMMAND_MASTER 0x4 "Enable bus mastering" (opened). Used in: layer3.
