C. Facts (keyed to the chapter's claim tags)

  1. F1 [R1] All 72 single-bit errors are corrected; all 2556 two-bit errors are detected as uncorrectable; of the 59640 three-bit errors, 45304 are "corrected" wrongly and 14336 are detected: SECDED protects against one flip, warns about two, and three or more can pass as a wrong correction. Used in: pictures (Figure 1), layer2, layer3, worked example tasks 1–3, mistakes, summary
  2. F2 [R2] Toy chip: dynamic power V²·f is 1.000 at level 0 (f 1.00, V 1.00), 0.648 at level 1 (0.80, 0.90), 0.384 at level 2 and 0.281 at level 3 (0.50, 0.75), so lowering both saves more than lowering f alone; the temperature climbs from 29.4 towards the limit, the controller steps down above 90 and back up below 85 (hysteresis), and the run averages 0.82 of full speed. Used in: pictures (Figure 2), layer2, worked example task 4, checks
  3. F3 [R3] Generated log: corrected errors at one location (channel 1, DIMM 0, rank 0, bank 3, row 0x1a2b, bit 17, syndrome 0x4b) recur day after day with growing daily counts (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) while other locations show isolated single entries; the pattern points at one weak cell/row, not at a noise floor. Used in: forensic pack and answer key
  4. F4 [R4] On the build VM /sys/class/thermal is empty and /sys/class/hwmon and /sys/devices/system/edac are absent; nothing real was read. Used in: untested-on-hardware box, lab
  5. F5 [D7] The catalog lists ACPI thermal zone names _TMP, _PSV, _CRT, _HOT, _TZP, the Intel digital thermal sensor registers and AMD per-family documents; warns not to override fan control until the zones are understood; names EDAC drivers ie31200_edac, skx_edac, i10nm_edac, amd64_edac; quotes "Suspend to RAM (S3) and Modern Standby (S0 low-power idle) need every driver to save and restore state: plan for it late"; milestone B2 gives machine checks a separate TSS stack. Used in: jargon, layer2, layer3, unverified box 1 (names only)
