Fact list

IdFact (paraphrased)SourceUsed in
F1A half-adder adds two bits and outputs sum and carry; a full-adder adds three bits; an n-bit adder adds two n-bit numbers.D1 §2.2.1, §2.3Jargon, Layer 1, Layer 2
F2A half adder is an XOR (sum) and an AND (carry); a full adder is two half adders and an OR gate; full adders chain carry-out to carry-in.D3 ch.14Figure 1, Figure 2, Layer 2
F3Adding n-bit numbers ripples the carry from the least significant bit; a carry-ripple adder is a chain of full adders.D3 ch.14; D7 L17Jargon (ripple-carry adder), Figure 3, Layer 2
F4A carry out of the most significant bit is discarded in fixed-width (two's complement) addition; the same adder serves signed and unsigned numbers.D8 §3.2.1.2Layer 2, width demo
F5Circuit delay is the longest path of cumulative tPD; in a ripple adder the carry path through every stage is that path.D8 §7.7Layer 3
F6Carry look-ahead is a faster adder design; D1's Add16 ignores overflow and leaves faster adders as an option.D1 §2.3, §2.4; D7 L17Layer 3, hardware section
F7abc -g maps a design to the listed gate types (here NAND, with NOT always added).D6 help abcGate-count paragraph (R4)
