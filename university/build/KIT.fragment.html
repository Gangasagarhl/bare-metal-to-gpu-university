This file is the reference kit required by owner ruling D1 (and the stack choices of D2-D3). Each item was checked on 2026-10-10 against the vendor's or project's own page, and each table row names that page. Prices change, so none are given; check the current price with the vendor. One primary item is chosen per need, with at most one alternative. Anything that could not be confirmed is listed under "Not verified" at the end. Units name kit items from this file (D1). Part B hardware steps refer to it, and C3 still applies: a hardware step that has not been run stays marked "untested on hardware".

Common host platform: Ubuntu 24.04 LTS (x86-64 PCs; arm64 on the Raspberry Pi robot computer). It is the one Linux release that every chosen stack lists. ROS 2 Jazzy has it as Tier 1 for amd64 and arm64. PX4 v1.17 supports it. The CUDA 13.4 Linux guide lists Ubuntu 24.04 LTS (amd64). ROCm 7.14.0 lists Ubuntu 24.04.4. The TurtleBot3 Jazzy setup uses Ubuntu Server 24.04.2 on the Raspberry Pi. The sources are the rows below.

Teaching stand-ins (ruling A2): every page that uses a course-own model must say so and name the real item it stands for. Use the items here as the real names: the fictional U-MCU-1 / U-BOARD-1 and the QEMU mps2-an385 / stm32vldiscovery models stand for the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 (RP2350, Cortex-M33) or the NUCLEO-F446RE; the course's quadrotor and drone simulators stand for PX4 SITL with Gazebo Harmonic (x500 model) and ArduPilot SITL; the course's house and robot simulators stand for Gazebo Harmonic with ROS 2 Jazzy and the TurtleBot3 model; uRTOS stands for Zephyr and FreeRTOS; the virtual cluster stands for the two-node PC-A/PC-B cluster with ConnectX-6 Lx NICs.

Safety notes

LiPo batteries (robot 3S pack, drone 4S packs)

  • Follow the battery maker's and charger maker's instructions; they override anything here.
  • Charge only with a balance charger in LiPo mode, at or below the pack's rated charge current, inside a LiPo bag or metal box, on a non-flammable surface, never unattended.
  • Never charge, fly or drive with a pack that is puffed, dented, punctured or warm without reason; take it out of use and dispose of it as the maker and local rules say.
  • Store packs at the maker's storage voltage, in the bag, away from heat; transport with terminals covered.
  • Kids' kits (KID103, RB101) use AA cells only, never LiPo (RB101 decision box; guide 5.14).

Propellers and motors

  • Propellers stay OFF for every bench test, firmware flash, parameter change, motor test and HITL session (F10 safety box).
  • Motors and propellers get no power before gate R3 is signed (rulings B1, B2), and R3 is signed in person by the supervisor of real hardware runs.
  • Configure and test the arming switch and failsafes (RC loss, low battery, geofence) in SITL first, then on the bench with props off.
  • First hover tethered or netted (MP7 M4b), with a marked clear zone and eye protection for everyone present.
  • Outdoor flight only where the national aviation authority's current rules allow it, with land-owner permission.

Robots

  • Simulator lab passes first, then the real robot (F9 safety box).
  • An e-stop within reach for every real run; ruling D4: the robot image refuses to arm while the e-stop reads pressed.
  • Speed limits configured before the first run; clear zone; fingers away from wheels and arm joints; the arm runs at reduced speed until its limits are tested.

Firmware, kernels and boards

  • QEMU (with OVMF for UEFI) first; real hardware only on the designated spare PC or board, never on a machine the learner depends on.
  • Keep a recovery path: SD-card boot (Raspberry Pi 4, VisionFive 2), the RP2350 USB boot mode, a backup image.

Supervision

  • Adult supervision for every physical lab below L3 and for every drone session for younger learners (F9, F10 safety boxes).
  • Mains-powered equipment (PC power supplies, chargers) is never opened; kids' labs are battery-only and low voltage.
  • SE404 safeguarding minimum (ruling B5) applies to any mentored hardware session with a minor.
